# 🎨 Texas: Fractured Time — Art Guide

This guide has everything you need to make the game's art with an AI image tool. The goal is to match the look we already have: a **cinematic storybook**, not just a watercolor painting.

---

## 🧭 How to use this guide (4 steps)

1. **Pick a picture** from the **Art Checklist** (Part 8). Start with the ⭐ **MVP** list.
2. **Upload the reference picture(s)** if your AI tool allows it (Part 0). For people, this matters more than anything you type.
3. **Build your prompt** by copying these blocks, in this order:
   1. 🎬 **Style Spine** (Part 1). Always first.
   2. 👧 **Character block(s)** (Parts 2–3), if people are in the picture.
   3. 🏞️ **Scene Builder** (Part 5), with the blanks filled in.
   4. 🖼️ **Format block** (Part 6).
   5. 🚫 **Avoid list** (Part 1). Paste it at the end, or into the "negative prompt" box.
4. **Save it.** Put all your tries in `art/drafts/`. Save the best one using the file name in the checklist, in the folder shown, and tick the box ✅. (More help: [`art/README.md`](art/README.md).)

> ✏️ **Only change words inside `[square brackets]`.** Everything else keeps the art consistent, so leave it alone.

**The three rules of references**
1. 🖼️ **Always upload the girl's reference** when she's in the picture. If two girls are in it, upload both.
2. 🏆 **The reference beats the words.** If the picture matches the words but not the reference, it's wrong. Make it again.
3. 🔁 **Never make a new picture from a new picture.** Always go back to the locked reference, or small changes add up and she slowly turns into someone else.

> 💡 If your tool doesn't take reference images, paste the character block word-for-word, make 4 versions, and keep the one closest to the reference.

## 1. 🎬 Style Spine

### Style Spine: paste this first, every time
```
Cinematic storybook illustration for a historical adventure game set in 1830s Texas, like a frame from a hand-painted animated film. Confident ink linework with visible pencil sketch lines underneath, rich watercolor and gouache washes with soft bleeding edges, painted on warm textured cream paper. Expressive characters whose faces and body language show what they feel without words. Environmental storytelling: small details like footprints, scattered belongings, weather and smoke hint at what just happened. Carefully composed like a film shot: one clear focal point, framing elements in the foreground, depth through layers of atmosphere. Dramatic but natural lighting: golden sunlight, long shadows, glowing lanterns, cool indigo shade. Earthy palette of dusty ochre, sienna brown, indigo, smoke gray, sage green, cream and small accents of muted vermilion. Historically grounded, emotional and human.
```

### 🚫 Avoid list: paste at the end (or into the "negative prompt" box)
```
Do not include: photorealism, photographs, 3D render, CGI, video-game screenshot, anime, chibi, cartoon mascots, flat vector art, glossy or plastic look, neon colors, lens flare, airbrushed digital look, stiff posed characters staring at the camera, empty generic backgrounds, modern objects, text, letters, words, captions, logos, signatures, watermarks, frames or borders, blood, gore, visible injuries.
```

> 💡 **Why no words in pictures?** AI tools spell badly. We add all titles, names and labels in the game itself.

## 2. 👧 Main Characters

**Rules for all three girls**
- About 12–15 years old. They look like **real kids**, not grown-ups.
- They wear their **research-team travel clothes**: made to blend into the 1830s.
- Each has an **animal symbol**: deer, horse or fox.
  - On the **character select screen**, the real animal is in the picture with her (see 2d).
  - **Everywhere else**, the animal only appears as her pendant or charm. Never animal ears, tails, costumes or magic powers.

### 2a. 👤 Silhouette test: can you tell them apart in the dark?

If you colored each girl solid black, you should **still know who is who**. Each girl has her own shape:

| | 🦌 Rowan | 🐴 Morgan | 🦊 Ellis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Big shape** | Tall, soft and **draped**: the hooded shawl makes a long cloak shape | **Broad, sturdy triangle**: square shoulders, wide stance, straps and vest | **Huge round cloud of curls** on a small, compact body |
| **Head shape** | Long wavy hair falling past her shoulders | **High bun** = a peak on top of the head | The biggest head shape of the three: the curls |
| **Posture** | Still, slightly leaning forward, head tilted as if listening | Upright, chin up, weight planted on both feet | Low and coiled: crouching, perched, leaning on walls |
| **Hands** | Close to her body: holding the pendant, the journal or the strap of her bag | Busy and strong: gripping, lifting, resting on a horse or a friend's shoulder | Talking with them: pointing, counting on fingers, fiddling with a compass |
| **How she moves** | Slowly and carefully, from the edge of a crowd | Straight toward the problem | Sideways, finding another way around |
| **Where she stands in a scene** | At the edge, watching | In front, between danger and others | Off to the side or above, scouting |
| **Eyes** | Soft gaze looking up or into the distance | Steady, looking straight ahead | Side glance, half-smile, always thinking |

> ✅ **Group shots:** never put two girls in the same pose. If all three are in a picture, Rowan watches, Morgan steps forward and Ellis looks for another way.

## 4. 🎥 Making It Cinematic

Our art isn't just pretty. Every picture is a **moment in a story**. Before you write a scene prompt, answer these three questions:

1. 🎯 **What's the focal point?** One thing the eye goes to first (a face, the cannon, the glowing device).
2. ⏪ **What just happened?** Show a clue: footprints, a dropped hat, smoke drifting, an open door.
3. ⏩ **What's about to happen?** Show tension: soldiers on the horizon, a rising river, a worried glance.

**Composition tricks** (add one or two to the ACTION or CAMERA line):
| Trick | Words to add |
|---|---|
| Frame the shot | `framed by dark foreground branches / a doorway / a wall edge` |
| Small people, big history | `tiny figures against a huge sky / fort / battlefield` |
| Lead the eye | `a road / river / fence line leading toward [the focal point]` |
| Light tells the story | `a single beam of golden light on [focal point], the rest in shadow` |
| Feelings first | `close enough to read the emotion on her face` |
| Depth | `layers of haze and smoke fading into the distance` |

## 5. 🏞️ Scene Builder

### Fill-in-the-blanks template
```
SCENE: [place], [date or season], [time of day and weather].
WHO: [paste character blocks here, or write "no main characters"].
STORY MOMENT: [what just happened] → [what is happening now] → [what is about to happen].
FOCAL POINT: [the one thing the eye should see first].
CAMERA: [pick from the camera word bank + a composition trick].
MOOD & LIGHT: [pick from the mood word bank].
```

### 📷 Camera word bank
| Shot | When to use it |
|---|---|
| `wide establishing shot` | The first picture of a new place |
| `medium shot, waist-up` | Two people talking |
| `close-up on hands` | Holding the device, pushing a wheel |
| `over-the-shoulder shot` | Seeing what a character sees |
| `low angle looking up` | Something big or scary |
| `high angle looking down` | A crowd, a river, a camp |
| `hidden view through grass/branches` | Sneaking and spying |

### 🌤️ Mood & light word bank
`warm golden sunset` · `cold blue dawn fog` · `gray rainy afternoon` · `candlelit night, deep indigo shadows` · `hazy smoky battlefield light` · `bright spring morning` · `quiet, sad, still evening` · `tense, moonlit night`

### ⭐ Example (G-01)
```
[STYLE SPINE]
SCENE: the small frontier town of Gonzales beside the Guadalupe River, early October 1835, late afternoon with a dusty golden sky.
WHO: no main characters; small groups of settlers in the middle distance.
STORY MOMENT: riders have just brought news that Mexican soldiers are coming → settlers gather around a small brass cannon in an ox cart → a white flag with a black cannon and star is about to be raised.
FOCAL POINT: the little cannon in the ox cart, lit by a beam of low sun.
CAMERA: wide establishing shot, framed by a dark cabin corner in the foreground, a dirt road leading toward the cannon.
MOOD & LIGHT: warm golden sunset, tense and quiet.
[FORMAT F1]
[AVOID LIST]
```

## 6. 🖼️ Format Blocks

Choose the **shape** in your AI tool too. Always pick the **biggest size** the tool allows and save as **PNG**.

| Code | Kind of picture | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Background scene (no main characters) | Landscape 3:2 |
| F2 | Character cutout | Portrait 2:3 |
| F3 | Expression sheet | Square 1:1 |
| F4 | Object / prop / icon | Square 1:1 |
| F5 | Full illustrated moment (with characters) | Landscape 3:2 |
| F6 | Foreground layer | Landscape 3:2 |
| F7 | Paper texture | Square 1:1 |
| F8 | Character select panel | Portrait 4:5 (or 2:3) |

**F1: Background scene**
```
FORMAT: a background scene with NO main characters in the front, leaving open space in the lower middle where characters will be added later. Wide landscape composition.
```

**F2: Character cutout**
```
FORMAT: [full body, standing / waist-up portrait] of ONE character in her own typical pose and body language, facing slightly to the [left / right], on a plain flat cream background with nothing else, so it can be cut out easily.
```

**F3: Expression sheet**
```
FORMAT: a 2-by-2 grid of the SAME character's head and shoulders, same outfit and same angle in every square, showing four expressions: top-left [neutral], top-right [happy], bottom-left [worried], bottom-right [determined]. Plain cream background.
```
> ✂️ After you make it, crop it into 4 separate squares and add `_neutral`, `_happy`, `_worried`, `_determined` to the end of the file names. Use the expression table in Part 2c to fill in each girl's version.

**F4: Object / prop / icon**
```
FORMAT: one single object, centered, drawn like a page in an explorer's sketchbook, on a plain cream paper background with nothing else.
```

**F5: Full illustrated moment**
```
FORMAT: a complete illustrated moment with the characters in the scene, like one frame of a hand-painted animated film. Wide landscape composition.
```

**F6: Foreground layer**
```
FORMAT: only [tall grass / oak branches and leaves / smoke wisps / brush and bushes / a broken wall edge] along the [bottom / left and right edges / top] of the picture, on a plain flat white background with nothing else, so it can be cut out and placed in front of other scenes.
```

**F7: Paper texture**
```
FORMAT: a flat, evenly lit, seamless texture of [warm cream rag paper / watercolor wash stains / torn paper edges], with no objects or drawings, filling the whole picture.
```

**F8: Character select panel**
```
FORMAT: a tall storybook panel showing ONE girl with her animal, painted with soft, ragged watercolor edges that fade into cream paper, like the reference image. No name, no words.
```

## 7. 📜 History Cheat-Sheet

Add a **location block** after `SCENE:` to help the AI. Then check your picture against the ✅ / ❌ tips.

> 🛡️ **Keep it PG.** Show what happened through faces, smoke, empty places and distance. Never show injuries or bodies.

**👕 Who wore what (1835–1836)**
- **Texian volunteers:** everyday frontier clothes (homespun shirts, frock coats, slouch hats, long rifles). Hardly anyone had a uniform.
- **Mexican army soldiers:** dark blue coats with red trim, white crossbelts, light trousers, tall black shako hats, muskets with bayonets.
- **Tejano families and riders:** cotton shirts, wide-brimmed hats, rebozo shawls, serapes, leather riding gear.
- ❌ No cowboy hats like in modern movies, no revolvers (they barely existed yet), no metal buildings or barbed wire.

| Place & date | Location block (paste after SCENE) | ✅ Get it right | ❌ Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌃 **Modern San Antonio** (Prologue) | `a quiet research site at night near old Spanish mission ruins, portable lights and equipment cases, the modern San Antonio skyline glowing far away` | Modern gear is OK only here | — |
| 🏘️ **Gonzales**, Oct 2 1835 | `the small frontier town of Gonzales on the Guadalupe River, log cabins and simple wooden houses, dirt roads, autumn grass` | The "Come and Take It" flag is **white with a black cannon and one black star** (we add the words in the game) | No stone buildings, no bluebonnets (wrong season) |
| 🏛️ **Béxar** (San Antonio), Dec 1835 | `the streets of San Antonio de Béxar in December 1835, flat-roofed stone and adobe houses, a dusty plaza, the stone church of San Fernando with its dome` | Cold winter: bare trees, people in shawls | No flowers, no tall buildings |
| 🏰 **The Alamo**, Feb–Mar 1836 | `the old Alamo mission in winter 1836, a roofless weathered stone church with a flat broken top edge, long adobe walls around a big dusty courtyard, wooden cannon platforms` | ⚠️ **The church's famous curved "hump" top was NOT there yet**; it was added in 1850. Make the top **flat and broken**. | No hump, no green lawns, no modern city |
| 📜 **Washington-on-the-Brazos**, Mar 2 1836 | `an unfinished wooden building with cloth covering the window holes, cold and drafty, men in frock coats around rough wooden tables with papers and ink` | It was freezing that day | No fancy marble hall |
| ⛪ **Goliad**, Mar 1836 | `the old stone fort Presidio La Bahía at Goliad with its small stone chapel and bell tower` | Quiet, sad, empty; smoke in the distance | No bodies, no violence shown |
| 🌧️ **Runaway Scrape**, Mar–Apr 1836 | `muddy roads in heavy rain, families fleeing east on foot, in ox carts and covered wagons, swollen brown rivers` | Show many kinds of families: Anglo settlers, Tejano families and enslaved Black people who were also forced to flee | Not snow, but cold rain and mud |
| 🌾 **San Jacinto**, Apr 21 1836 | `open prairie beside Buffalo Bayou with live oak trees and marshy grass, spring wildflowers, a Mexican army camp behind a low barricade of packs and saddles` | Afternoon battle, lots of smoke; bluebonnets are OK here (spring) | No mountains, no deserts with cactus |

## 9. 🛠️ Fixing Problems

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| A character looks different every time | Upload her **locked reference** (Part 0) and paste the **exact** character block. Make 4 versions and keep the closest. Never use a newer picture as the reference. |
| The three girls look too similar | Do the silhouette check (Part 2a). Add her body-language line again at the end of the prompt. |
| It looks like a flat watercolor, not cinematic | Add a FOCAL POINT, a framing trick and a light trick (Part 4). Add: `dramatic storybook lighting, like a still from a hand-painted animated film.` |
| The picture is pretty but boring | Answer the 3 questions in Part 4 and put the answers in STORY MOMENT. |
| Ellis's hair comes out tied up, or with orange tips | Add at the end: `Her curly hair is completely down and loose, natural dark brown all the way to the tips.` |
| Rowan's or Morgan's hairstyle is wrong | Repeat the hairstyle at the end: `Hair: half-up, half-down with thin braids.` or `Hair: messy high bun.` |
| The girls look too old | Add: `She looks like a real 13-year-old middle schooler, youthful face.` |
| The animal is missing or wrong on a select panel | Name it again at the end: `A white-tailed deer doe / chestnut horse with a white blaze / gray fox is clearly visible beside her.` |
| Animals show up in story scenes | Add `no deer, no fox` to the Avoid list. Horses are fine in history scenes. |
| It looks like a photo or 3D game | Move the Style Spine to the very start and add: `traditional ink and watercolor on paper, visible brush strokes.` |
| There are random words or letters | Add `no text, no letters` near the start as well as in the Avoid list. |
| The Alamo has a curved hump | Add: `the top of the church front is flat and broken, no curved parapet.` |
| Hands look weird | Hide them (in pockets, behind things) or make a separate close-up. |
| Too scary or too violent | Move the camera farther away and use smoke and silhouettes. Show feelings on faces, not injuries. |
| Colors are too bright | Add: `muted, earthy, faded watercolor colors.` |

**Before you tick ✅, check:**
- [ ] Looks like a **cinematic storybook** frame: hand-drawn ink and watercolor, with a clear focal point
- [ ] Tells a story moment (you can say what just happened)
- [ ] Matches the **locked reference**: hair, colors, pendant, face
- [ ] Passes the silhouette test: her shape and body language are her own
- [ ] No words, logos or watermarks
- [ ] Nothing modern (unless it's the prologue or the Paradox ending)
- [ ] Kept PG: no injuries or blood
- [ ] Saved as PNG with the right file name in the right folder
